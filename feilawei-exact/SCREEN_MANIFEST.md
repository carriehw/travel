## Rendering rule
- **Approved artwork = the visible screen.**
- No visible HTML/CSS illustration, text, cards, icons, borders, shadows, stickers, or decoration may be drawn over an approved screen.
- Web code may add only invisible interaction hotspots, navigation/state, analytics, logic, and native share actions.
- The artwork must be displayed without cropping or aspect-ratio distortion.
