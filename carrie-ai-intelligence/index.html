Andrej Karpathy joins Anthropic as it preps an IPO as early as October Andrej Karpathy 加盟 Anthropic，公司最快 10 月 IPO

Summary摘要

Former Tesla AI director and OpenAI founding member Andrej Karpathy has reportedly joined Anthropic. In the same week, Anthropic was reported to be in talks with Samsung on a custom Claude chip and preparing an S-1 for an IPO as early as October 2026, after a Series H valuing it at $965B. 前 Tesla AI 總監、OpenAI 創始成員 Andrej Karpathy 據報加盟 Anthropic。同一星期，Anthropic 據報同 Samsung 商討訂製 Claude 晶片，並準備最快 2026 年 10 月 IPO——公司喺 Series H 融資後估值已達 9,650 億美元。

Why it matters點解重要

Top talent, custom compute and public-market capital are consolidating around two or three frontier labs. Whoever your martech stack depends on, its roadmap is being decided now. 頂級人才、訂製算力同上市資金正集中喺兩三間 frontier labs 手上。無論你嘅 martech stack 依賴邊間公司，佢嘅未來路線圖而家就係決定緊。

Carrie's Take

Most people will read this as an AI-industry story. I read it as a procurement story: the enterprise AI market is becoming a two-horse race, and agencies that build on only one platform are taking on concentration risk their clients never signed off on. 大部分人會當呢單係 AI 行業新聞。我當佢係採購新聞：企業 AI 市場正變成兩強競賽，只押注一個平台嘅 agency，其實係替客戶承擔咗佢哋從未同意過嘅集中風險。

By mid-2027, "multi-model strategy" will appear in agency RFPs the same way "multi-cloud" appeared in IT RFPs five years ago. 到 2027 年中，「multi-model strategy」會好似五年前 IT 界嘅「multi-cloud」咁，開始出現喺 agency RFP 入面。
