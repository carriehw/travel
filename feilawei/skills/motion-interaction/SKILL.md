# Skill: Motion & Interaction

## Motion personality
Playful, light, happy, not hyperactive.

## Approved motion types
- mascot float / blink
- decorative sparkle
- dashed route movement
- option press / bounce
- blind-box wiggle
- countdown
- reveal pop
- confetti
- gentle page transition

## Rules
- animation must not alter final layout coordinates
- 150–350ms for tap feedback
- page transitions generally <= 450ms
- reveal sequence may be longer for suspense
- preserve 60fps where possible
- respect prefers-reduced-motion
